❓ What causes Amazon price errors and do orders get cancelled?

Price errors happen when third-party sellers make algorithmic mistakes in dynamic repricing tools or during promotional catalog updates. While Amazon occasionally cancels severe glitches (like 99% off iPhones), 80%+ of modest price-drop errors get successfully dispatched if ordered immediately.
